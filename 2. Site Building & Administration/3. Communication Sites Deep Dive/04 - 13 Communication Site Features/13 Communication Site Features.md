# Communication Site Features

Canonical documentation for Communication Site Features. This document defines concepts, terminology, and standard usage.

## Purpose

The purpose of Communication Site Features is to provide a centralized platform for sharing information, news, and resources across an organization. This topic exists to address the problem space of effective communication and collaboration within a company, where employees, teams, and departments need to stay informed and up-to-date on various topics, projects, and initiatives. The goal is to facilitate knowledge sharing, improve engagement, and enhance overall organizational efficiency. This documentation is intended to be implementation-agnostic and authoritative, focusing on the underlying concepts and principles rather than specific technical implementations.

> [!NOTE]
> This documentation is intended to be implementation-agnostic and authoritative.

## Scope

The scope of Communication Site Features includes the design, development, and deployment of features that enable effective communication and collaboration within an organization. The following aspects are in scope:

**In scope:**
* Site structure and navigation
* Content creation and management
* User engagement and feedback mechanisms
* Integration with other collaboration tools and platforms

**Out of scope:**
* Tool-specific implementations (e.g., SharePoint, WordPress)
* Vendor-specific behavior (e.g., Microsoft, Google)
* Custom development and coding practices

## Core Concepts

The core concepts of Communication Site Features include:

### Site Structure and Navigation
A well-organized site structure and intuitive navigation are essential for users to find and access relevant information quickly. This includes the use of clear headings, categorization, and search functionality.

### Content Creation and Management
Effective content creation and management involve the development of high-quality, engaging content that meets the needs of the target audience. This includes content types, such as news articles, blog posts, and documents, as well as content management practices, like editing, reviewing, and publishing.

### User Engagement and Feedback
User engagement and feedback mechanisms are critical for encouraging participation, improving content quality, and enhancing overall user experience. This includes features like comments, ratings, and surveys, as well as analytics and reporting tools to track engagement metrics.

## Standard Model

The standard model for Communication Site Features involves a centralized platform with the following components:

* A clear site structure and navigation
* A content management system for creating, editing, and publishing content
* User engagement and feedback mechanisms, such as comments and ratings
* Integration with other collaboration tools and platforms, like email and instant messaging

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Deviations from the standard model should be explicitly documented and justified.

## Common Patterns

Common patterns associated with Communication Site Features include:

* Using a templated approach to content creation and management
* Implementing a content approval workflow to ensure quality and consistency
* Utilizing analytics and reporting tools to track engagement metrics and inform content strategy
* Integrating with other collaboration tools and platforms to enhance user experience

## Anti-Patterns

Anti-patterns to avoid when implementing Communication Site Features include:

* Overly complex site structures and navigation
* Insufficient content management practices, leading to outdated or low-quality content
* Lack of user engagement and feedback mechanisms, resulting in low participation and engagement
* Inadequate integration with other collaboration tools and platforms, leading to siloed information and workflows

> [!WARNING]
> These anti-patterns often lead to maintenance or scalability issues, negatively impacting user experience and overall organizational efficiency.

## Edge Cases

Edge cases to consider when implementing Communication Site Features include:

* Handling sensitive or confidential information, such as employee personal data or financial reports
* Supporting multiple languages and regional preferences
* Accommodating users with disabilities, such as visual or hearing impairments
* Integrating with external systems or services, like social media or customer relationship management (CRM) platforms

> [!CAUTION]
> Edge cases are frequently overlooked and may cause incorrect assumptions or inadequate solutions.
