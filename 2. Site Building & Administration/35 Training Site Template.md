# Training Site Template

Canonical documentation for Training Site Template. This document defines concepts, terminology, and standard usage.

## Purpose

The Training Site Template exists to provide a standardized framework for creating and delivering online training content. It addresses the problem space of inconsistent and disorganized training materials, which can lead to confusion, decreased learner engagement, and reduced knowledge retention. The Training Site Template aims to establish a common structure and set of best practices for designing and implementing effective online training sites, thereby improving the overall learning experience and increasing the efficiency of training programs.

> [!NOTE]
> This documentation is intended to be implementation-agnostic and authoritative.

## Scope

The scope of the Training Site Template includes the design, development, and deployment of online training sites. The following concepts are in scope:

**In scope:**
* Learning Objectives: defining the goals and outcomes of the training program
* Content Organization: structuring and categorizing training content
* User Experience: designing an intuitive and engaging interface for learners
* Assessment and Evaluation: measuring learner progress and understanding

**Out of scope:**
* Tool-specific implementations: such as learning management system (LMS) configurations or authoring tool workflows
* Vendor-specific behavior: including proprietary features or functionality of specific training platforms

## Definitions

The following terms are used throughout this documentation:

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Learning Object | A self-contained unit of training content, such as a lesson or module |
| Learning Path | A sequence of learning objects designed to achieve a specific learning objective |
| Assessment | A evaluation of a learner's knowledge or skills, such as a quiz or exam |
| Certification | A formal recognition of a learner's completion of a training program or achievement of a specific skill level |

> [!TIP]
> Definitions should be stable over time; avoid contextual language.

## Core Concepts

The Training Site Template is based on the following core concepts:

### Learning Objectives
Learning objectives define the specific skills or knowledge that learners are expected to acquire through the training program. They provide a clear direction for the design and development of the training content.

### Content Organization
Content organization refers to the structure and categorization of the training content. This includes the creation of learning objects, learning paths, and other navigational elements that facilitate learner progression.

### User Experience
The user experience is critical to the success of the training program. It encompasses the design of the interface, the layout of the content, and the overall usability of the training site.

## Standard Model

The standard model for the Training Site Template consists of the following components:

1. **Welcome Page**: an introductory page that provides an overview of the training program and its objectives
2. **Learning Paths**: a series of learning objects that are organized to achieve a specific learning objective
3. **Assessments**: evaluations of learner knowledge or skills that are used to measure progress and understanding
4. **Resources**: supplementary materials that support the learning process, such as job aids or reference guides
5. **Certification**: a formal recognition of learner completion of the training program or achievement of a specific skill level

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Deviations from the standard model should be explicitly documented and justified.

## Common Patterns

The following patterns are commonly associated with the Training Site Template:

* **Linear Learning Path**: a sequence of learning objects that are designed to be completed in a linear fashion
* **Branching Learning Path**: a sequence of learning objects that allow learners to choose their own path or make decisions that affect the learning outcome
* **Microlearning**: a approach to learning that involves short, focused learning objects that are designed to be completed in a short period of time

## Anti-Patterns

The following anti-patterns are discouraged in the context of the Training Site Template:

* **Information Overload**: presenting too much information at once, which can lead to learner confusion and decreased engagement
* **Lack of Feedback**: failing to provide learners with feedback on their progress or performance, which can lead to frustration and decreased motivation
* **Inconsistent Navigation**: using inconsistent navigation or layout, which can lead to learner confusion and decreased usability

> [!WARNING]
> These anti-patterns often lead to maintenance or scalability issues.

## Edge Cases

The following edge cases should be considered when implementing the Training Site Template:

* **Learner Variability**: accommodating learners with different learning styles, abilities, or languages
* **Technical Issues**: handling technical issues, such as browser compatibility or connectivity problems
* **Content Updates**: managing updates to the training content, such as changes to regulations or policies

> [!CAUTION]
> Edge cases are frequently overlooked and may cause incorrect assumptions.
